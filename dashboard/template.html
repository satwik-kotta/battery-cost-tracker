    Limits

    Lithium carbonate is inferred from the IMF lithium-metal series, not observed. Costs here are market-price replacement costs: automakers buy under contracts with lags and hedges, so what they pay moves later and less. Pack sizes and chemistry shares by vehicle are compiled by hand from public specifications. Several non-metal inputs are author assumptions, each labelled in the assumptions files.
